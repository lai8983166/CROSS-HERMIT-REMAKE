# Proposal

## Why

The graphical playground currently stops when fourth-week courses and dialogue reach 4/5. Players need the actual date-selected fifth-week scene and a durable continuation beyond the arrival card.

## What Changes

- Execute CH001's 4/5 branch and Chapter018 in the same isolated CPU as the verified course/story/week predecessor; record completion and readiness waits.
- Export the original Chapter018 dialogue, background, large character art and small portrait replacements with strict source hashes.
- Add once-only fifth-week reading and its verified state8/workroom handoff, preserving fourth-week results and migrating version1/2 saves.
- Connect arrival, reader and workroom-entry summary in the default graphical playground, with automated mouse/keyboard, save/load and rendered acceptance.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `school/fifth-week-story`: Source-selected fifth-week dialogue, mixed portrait presentation, durable progress and bounded workroom-entry handoff.

### Modified Capabilities

None. Existing main specs concern assets and battle simulation; prior school change specs remain available as context.

## Impact

Native audit tooling, separate fifth-week asset catalog and evidence/rules, playground controller/save schema, dialogue reader and arrival UI, tests and scope documentation. School construction, fifth-week course settlement, movie/audio fidelity and a chronological full campaign remain outside this change.
